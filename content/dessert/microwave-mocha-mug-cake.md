# Microwave Mocha Mug Cake
It's a mug cake recipe. For when you crave cake, but can't be bothered to make a whole one, or go and buy a deep-n-delicious one from the store.  

## Source
Yvonne Ruperti (Serious Eats)- [Microwave Mocha Mug Cake Recipe](https://www.seriouseats.com/microwave-mocha-mug-cake)

## Time
Prep time: 5 mins  
Cook time: 5 mins  
Total time: 10 mins  

## Ingredients
~~~~
+ 1     tbsp    unsalted butter, softened, plus extra for greasing mug
+ 3     tbsps   brown sugar
+ 3     tbsps   all purpose flour
+ 2     tbsps   cocoa
+ 1     tsp     instant coffee granules
+       Pinch   salt
+       Pinch   baking soda
+ 5     tbsps   water
+ ¼     tsp     vanilla extract
+ 2     tbsps   bittersweet chocolate chips (or 1 ounce chopped) (optional)

+               Ice cream or whipped cream to serve on top (optional)
~~~~

## Directions
Butter the inside of a mug. Add all of the ingredients (except for the "serve on top" option) to the mug and stir until completely combined. 

Wipe any excess batter from the inside rim of the cup. Microwave on high power until the cake has risen and looks moist (not wet) on top, checking every 30 seconds for 1 to 2 minutes. Do not overcook, or it will be too dry.

Let cool, then top with ice cream or whipped cream, and eat!

## Yield
1 serving (a mugful of cake)
